You may download, run, read and change Agent Arcade for free when you use it for yourself and not for business:

## Agent Arcade Pro: professional use, EUR 25 per user per month (excl. VAT)

One rule: if you're paid for the work, choose Pro. You need a Pro subscription when Agent Arcade is part of paid or organisational work, for example:

- you use it at or for your employer
- you use it for paid client work, as a freelancer or an agency
- you build or run a commercial product or service with it

When a side project starts earning, you have 30 days to switch to Pro. The phone remote follows the license of the PC it's paired with.

Pro is per person, not per PC, and includes the Agent Arcade voice dictation app for Windows (local speech-to-text tuned for talking to coding agents), which is not part of the free version.

Not sure which one applies to you? Ask in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/danular/AgentArcade/discussions) or open an issue.
