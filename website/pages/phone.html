Pairing

Paired in 30 seconds

  1. On the PC: Agent Arcade → Phone at the bottom of the sidebar → switch on Let my phone watch and drive these agents. If Windows asks, allow Agent Arcade on private networks.
  2. On the phone: tap Scan QR code and point it at the PC screen.
  3. Check the fingerprint. The PC and the phone show the same one. Tap Pair.

What it does

Everything you'd do at the desk, inside the panes

Needs you, first

Agents that need you are listed first, with how long they've waited and how far their plan has got ("Plan 3/7").

The live terminal

Open any agent to read its terminal live, in colour, with on-screen Esc, Tab, arrow, Enter and Ctrl+C keys.

One-tap answers

Numbered and yes/no prompts become buttons. Tap "1 Yes" and the pane on your PC carries on.

Start, stop, resume

Start an agent in any of your workspaces, stop one, resume a chat or rename it.

Alerts

Get an alert when an agent needs your OK, straight from your PC. Turn on "Stay connected" for alerts while the app is closed.

Your PC's view stays put

Nothing you do on the phone switches workspaces, moves focus or opens a dialog on your PC.

Security

Your phone talks to your PC. Nothing else.

  • Off until you switch it on, and the PC listens only while it's on.
  • Local network and Tailscale only. Every other address is dropped before the TLS handshake, even if a port is forwarded.
  • Pinned TLS 1.2/1.3. The phone trusts only your PC's own certificate, checked by its SHA-256 fingerprint.
  • One-time pairing codes that expire after 10 minutes and are cancelled after 5 wrong tries.
  • Your PC keeps only hashes of phone tokens. Remove a phone and its access stops at once.
  • No files, no settings, no logins. The phone can do only what you could do inside the panes.
Honest limits. Android only for now (8.0 and up). Away from home you need Tailscale on both devices; its Personal plan is free for non-commercial use. There's no cloud push service, so alerts while the app is closed need "Stay connected" and unrestricted battery use. Status detection reads the agents' English terminal text, so treat it as a strong signal, not a guarantee.
