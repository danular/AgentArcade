Pricing

Two plans and one rule

If you're paid for the work, choose Pro. Everyone else uses Agent Arcade for free, with every feature, for as long as they like.

Personal

EUR 0 no account, no key, no time limit

For you, for yourself: learning, hobby projects, unpaid open-source work and your own content. Charities, schools and other noncommercial organisations too.

Billing

The fine print, in plain words

Will Agent Arcade stop working if I don't pay?

No. Agent Arcade works with no key and no sign-in, and never checks a licence. Commercial licensing works on trust. Only {{voice}} will need a Pro key.

What will prices include?

Prices will exclude VAT. Dutch consumers will pay EUR 30.25 per month (EUR 302.50 per year) including 21% VAT; your country's VAT will be calculated at checkout. Businesses with a valid EU VAT ID will pay no VAT (reverse charge).

Will I be able to pay yearly?

Yes: EUR 250 per user per year, excl. VAT. That's two months free.

How will I cancel?

Online, any time, in the customer portal. Pro will stay active until the end of the period you paid for. Consumers will keep their legal 14-day right of withdrawal.

We need 10+ seats or want to pay by invoice.

Ask in Discussions and we'll set it up when checkout opens.

Students, teachers and open-source maintainers?

Agent Arcade is already free for you. Free Pro for coursework, teaching and unpaid open-source work is planned, so you can get {{voice}} too when it's ready; ask in Discussions.
