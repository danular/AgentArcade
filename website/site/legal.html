Privacy

The app. Agent Arcade collects nothing about you. It has no telemetry, no analytics and no account. Settings, the agent list, chat history (titles, folders and times only; a title can be the start of your first message) and paired phones are stored on your PC in %LOCALAPPDATA%\AgentArcade. The phone remote sends agent screens and your typing only between your phone and your PC, with no Agent Arcade server in between; over Tailscale, Tailscale's DERP servers may relay the encrypted traffic. From 1.3.3 the app's window (Microsoft WebView2) makes no SmartScreen lookups and keeps its crash dumps on your PC. What each agent sends to its vendor is between you and that vendor. See What leaves your PC.

This website. It sets no cookies and uses no trackers. The host's server logs (including IP addresses) are kept only as long as the host needs them for security. Downloads come from GitHub, under GitHub's privacy statement.
