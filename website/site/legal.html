License

Agent Arcade is source-available under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0. You may use, read and change it for free for personal and other noncommercial purposes. Professional (commercial) use needs a Pro subscription. LICENSING.md explains which applies, and Do I need Pro? gives examples.

The software is provided as is, without warranty. AI agents you run in Agent Arcade aren't sandboxed: they act with your Windows permissions and can change or delete files. You're responsible for what you let them do.

Privacy

The app. Agent Arcade collects nothing about you. It has no telemetry, no analytics and no account. Settings, the agent list, chat history (titles, folders and times only) and paired phones are stored on your PC in %LOCALAPPDATA%\AgentArcade. The phone remote connects your phone to your PC directly. What each agent sends to its vendor is between you and that vendor. See What leaves your PC.

This website. It sets no cookies and uses no trackers. The host's server logs (including IP addresses) are kept only as long as the host needs them for security. Downloads come from GitHub, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Pro. When Pro checkout opens, payments, VAT and invoices are handled by our reseller (Merchant of Record), which is a separate controller of your billing data. Agent Arcade Voice will check your licence key at most once a day. This section will name the reseller and list retention periods before checkout opens.

Questions or requests about your data: ask through GitHub Discussions, or privately through a private advisory if it's sensitive. You can also complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Terms of sale

Pro is a subscription per person: EUR 25 per user per month or EUR 250 per user per year, excluding VAT. It renews until you cancel, which you can do online at any time; Pro stays active until the end of the paid period. Consumers in the EU keep their 14-day right of withdrawal. Full terms are published here before checkout opens.
